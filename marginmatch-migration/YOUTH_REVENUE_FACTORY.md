# Youth Revenue Factory

Audience: adults 18+ and general-audience youth culture. Do not target or profile
minors for purchases, do not collect unnecessary minor data, and do not use
manipulative engagement mechanics.

Evidence-led themes to test:
- creator tools / personal-brand utilities
- gaming companion utilities that do not involve gambling or cheating
- social-commerce discovery / price-value comparison
- affordable "little treat" discovery
- fandom / micro-community utilities
- career / side-hustle productivity tools for adults 18+

Automated experiment queue:

1. TrendDrop — daily personalized trend digest + product/creator discovery.
   Revenue: affiliate/referral + optional premium filters.
2. CreatorKit AI — hooks, captions, content calendars, sponsorship media-kit drafts.
   Revenue: subscription / one-off digital packs.
3. DupeScout — value/alternative finder for fashion, beauty and accessories.
   Revenue: affiliate commissions; never counterfeit recommendations.
4. GameNight AI — game discovery, squad challenge generator, non-wagering tournaments,
   session recaps and share cards. Revenue: ads/affiliate/premium.
5. FanPulse — personalized release/drop/event digest for selected fandoms.
   Revenue: affiliate/referral + premium alerts.
6. SideQuest Studio — adult 18+ side-project generator, simple storefront copy,
   pricing experiments and launch checklists. Revenue: subscription/digital products.
7. CampusValue (18+ only) — student discounts, free events, cheap food/activity
   discovery using public offers. Revenue: referral/affiliate/local sponsored placement.
8. OutfitRemix — user-entered wardrobe/style constraints -> outfit ideas and
   shopping gaps. Revenue: affiliate/referral.
9. GiftDrop — automated low-budget gift finder for friends/partners.
   Revenue: affiliate.
10. CreatorSponsor Match — public-brand opportunity matching and email pitch drafting.
    Revenue: subscription or success fee where contractually appropriate.

Factory requirements:
- trend inputs are refreshed; never assume a trend is still hot;
- every experiment has attributable revenue and variable cost;
- no autonomous uncapped ad spend;
- no deceptive AI personas or fake UGC/reviews;
- respect platform automation/affiliate rules;
- email opt-outs and suppression are mandatory;
- experiments aimed at purchases default to adults 18+;
- scale only on contribution profit, not views.
